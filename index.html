Platform Features

Everything you need for remote care

Built for real-world healthcare challenges in low-resource settings, without sacrificing quality or security.

📹

Video, Audio & Text

Patients and providers connect through WebRTC-powered video and audio calls, or via live text chat when bandwidth is limited.

📋

Appointment Scheduling

Patients browse available providers, choose a time slot, and receive instant confirmation — all without a phone call.

👤

Role-Based Access

Separate workflows and dashboards for patients, healthcare providers, and administrators keep the experience clear and secure.

🔒

Privacy-First Security

HTTPS/TLS in transit, DTLS-SRTP for media, JWT authentication, and strict role-based data access keep patient records protected.

🌍

Low-Bandwidth Friendly

A lightweight interface designed to work reliably on slow or mobile connections common in remote and rural communities.

🛠️

Fully Open Source

Deploy, adapt, and extend TeleCare Connect for any organisation under the permissive MIT License — at zero cost.

How It Works

Get care in four simple steps

From sign-up to consultation in minutes — no specialist hardware needed.

1

Create an Account

Register as a patient or healthcare provider, verify your email, and set up your profile.

2

Book a Consultation

Browse available providers, pick a consultation type (video, audio, or text), and select a time slot.

3

Join Your Session

At the scheduled time, click Join from your appointments dashboard — no downloads required.

4

Receive Your Care

Consult with your provider, receive notes to your patient record, and book follow-ups as needed.
